# Ristorante Basilik — house rules

Source: `demo/Basilik_Policy.pdf`, August 2026 revision. These rules are here to answer
guests' questions. They are NOT a data source for bookings: opening hours, free tables and
prices always come from the tool result. If a tool and this text disagree, the tool wins.
Anything not covered here is never invented — offer a call back from the manager instead.

## Reservations

- A table can be booked no later than two hours before the visit.
- Banquets from 15 people: book at least three days ahead so the kitchen can plan
  purchasing and seating.
- A booking counts as confirmed only after the guest receives a confirmation. The request
  alone is not yet a guarantee of a table.
- Groups from eight people: a deposit of €15 per person, credited against the final bill.
- The table is held for 15 minutes from the booked time. If a guest is more than
  15 minutes late, the restaurant contacts them directly; until then the table stays
  reserved, but not indefinitely when the restaurant is full.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

- Free cancellation up to three hours before the booked time.
- For later cancellations the deposit of groups from eight people is not refunded: the
  kitchen and the table are already prepared for that number of guests.
- Moving a booking to another time or date is free of charge if notified at least three
  hours in advance.

## Reminders and confirmation

- Two hours before the booking a reminder is sent automatically, asking the guest to
  confirm the visit.
- If the guest does not reply, the manager contacts them personally.

## Banquets and large groups

- From 15 people: a separate banquet menu and a closed-off area of the dining room.
- Details are agreed individually with the manager after the initial request.
- The minimum spend and the menu depend on the number of guests and the date — only the
  manager quotes concrete figures.
- Payment: a 30% deposit on the preliminary order total no later than two days before the
  event, the balance on the day of the visit.

## Kids menu

- Available during all opening hours, no separate booking required.
- A high chair is provided if the guest requests one; do not ask about it proactively.

## Business lunch

- Weekdays from 12:00 to 16:00.
- Includes soup of the day, a main course of choice and a drink for a fixed price of €28.
- No booking is needed for the business lunch: during those hours tables are set aside for
  fast service on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Payment

- Cash, Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
- The bill can be split across several cards on request; there is no limit on the number
  of cards.
- Tips are not included in the bill and are left to the guest — in cash or as a separate
  card transaction.

## Bringing your own wine (corkage)

- Guests may bring their own wine. A corkage fee of €15 per bottle applies and covers
  glasses and service.
- Spirits and beer may not be brought in.

## Dress code

- There is no strict dress code; tidy smart casual is expected.
- No special requirements apply to the business lunch or an ordinary visit.
- For private banquets the format is agreed with the organiser.

## Pets

- Small pets are welcome on the summer terrace, on a lead or in a carrier.
- Pets are not allowed in the main dining room, with the exception of guide dogs.

## Opening hours, address and parking

- Daily from 12:00 to 23:00. The kitchen closes one hour before the dining room.
- The restaurant is located in Berlin.
- There is no dedicated car park. Street parking and a nearby public car park are
  available.
